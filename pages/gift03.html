If I Were There Right Now…

Don't read all 18 at once. Move through them slowly. Each one is a tiny moment that would happen if distance suddenly disappeared.

THE FIRST SECOND
01
IF I WERE
THERE

First thing?

I would come straight to you.

No dramatic entrance. No perfect dialogue. Just me finally standing in front of you after missing you for way too long.

DON'T LOOK AWAY
02
IF I WERE
THERE

Then I would just look at you.

Probably for a few seconds too long.

Because after talking through a screen for so long, seeing you standing right there would feel completely different.

CAUGHT
03
IF I WERE
THERE

Then...

I'd smile like an idiot.

Not because you said anything funny. Just because you are finally actually there. And yes, you would probably make fun of me for it.

STAY CLOSE
04
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd steal your hand.

And I wouldn't give it back easily.

Just walking beside you while holding your hand would probably feel strangely special after all the distance.

NO PLAN
05
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd ask one very important question.

“Where are we going?”

And whatever your answer was, I'd probably say yes. Because the point wouldn't really be the place.

LET'S JUST GO
06
IF I WERE
THERE

We'd go somewhere random.

No perfect itinerary.

Maybe we'd walk around, stop somewhere interesting, notice something completely stupid and somehow turn it into our own little memory.

MISSION: SMILE
07
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd make you laugh.

Or at least try very hard.

And if my joke was terrible, I would still defend it like it was the funniest thing ever created.

QUIET
08
IF I WERE
THERE

Then we'd have a quiet moment.

No phones. No notifications.

Just sitting somewhere together and letting the silence exist without needing to fill it.

HERE WE GO
09
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd probably annoy you.

Because obviously.

A peaceful reunion cannot survive too long without me doing something stupid.

COMPETITION
10
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd challenge you to something.

And absolutely pretend I'm going to win.

Whether it was a game, a silly challenge or something completely unnecessary, I would take it way too seriously.

KEEP THIS
11
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd take a picture.

Not for anyone else.

Just because I would want one ordinary picture of us actually being together instead of another screen full of distance.

YOUR CHOICE
12
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd ask you to choose something.

Anywhere. Anything.

Because I would want to see what a completely normal day with you feels like when we don't have to count the kilometres between us.

I'M LISTENING
13
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd listen to you.

Properly.

All the random things you wanted to tell me. The tiny things. The things you forgot halfway through telling me. All of it.

FUTURE MEMORY
14
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd make one memory unnecessarily dramatic.

Because we need a story to laugh about later.

Something completely normal would somehow become “remember when we did THAT?”

NOT YET
15
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd stay a little longer.

Even when it was time to go.

Because after waiting so long to be together, leaving would be the one part of the day I wouldn't want.

JUST THE TRUTH
16
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd tell you something simple.

“I'm really happy I'm here.”

No huge speech. Just the truth.

ONE MORE TIME
17
IF I WERE
THERE

I'd look at you one more time.

And probably smile again.

Because even after spending the whole day with you, I know I'd still have that stupid “I can't believe you're actually here” feeling.

STAY
18
IF I WERE
THERE

And finally...

I'd say: “Okay. Now don't go anywhere.”

Because if I were there right now, I wouldn't want the moment to end.
