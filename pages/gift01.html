18 Reasons I Love You

18 little notes. 18 very specific reasons. One very specific girl.

01

Because you became my favourite person without even trying.

I don't know exactly when it happened. Somewhere between our random talks, stupid jokes and normal days, you just became my person.

02

Because talking to you never feels like a normal conversation.

We can start with one topic and somehow reach five completely unrelated topics. And honestly, I don't even want to fix that.

03

Because you make me laugh at things that aren't even supposed to be funny.

Sometimes you say something so random that I just sit there thinking, ‘How did your brain even reach there?’

04

Because you know how to annoy me and still remain my favourite.

That is actually a very dangerous talent. Most people would get blocked. You somehow get more love.

05

Because you are not perfect, and I don't want you to be.

I like your weird little habits, your random moods, your silly moments and all the things that make you you.

06

Because even distance couldn't make you feel far away.

Gandhinagar and Surat are not exactly next door, but somehow you still became part of my everyday life.

07

Because sometimes one message from you can completely change my mood.

You probably don't even realise when you do it. And that's one of my favourite things about you.

08

Because I can be completely stupid with you.

I don't always have to be serious, mature or the ‘I have everything under control’ version of myself. With you, I can just be Zeel.

09

Because you somehow became part of my future without asking for the position.

When I think about things I want to build, experience and remember later, your name somehow keeps appearing in that story.

10

Because you tolerate my crazy ideas.

Normal people would probably ask, ‘Why are you doing all this?’ You somehow became part of the madness.

11

Because I love the way our relationship has its own little universe.

Our jokes, our words, our silly arguments, our random conversations — things that probably make absolutely no sense to anyone else.

12

Because you can make an ordinary day feel like something worth remembering.

Nothing huge has to happen. Sometimes it's just us talking, and somehow that's enough.

13

Because you make me want to keep memories.

Photos, messages, little moments, random conversations — suddenly even the smallest things feel worth saving.

14

Because I don't have to explain every version of myself to you.

You have seen the serious me, the overthinking me, the stupid me and the ‘I have another crazy idea’ me. And you're still here.

15

Because you became someone I genuinely care about, not just someone I love.

I care about your happiness, your future, your little problems and even the things you think are not important.

16

Because somehow, after everything, I still get excited to talk to you.

That part hasn't become normal. And I hope it never does.

17

Because if I had to choose my favourite person to annoy for the next many years…

Well, unfortunately for you, the position is already taken. No applications are being accepted.

18

Because it's you.

I could write 18 reasons today, and probably another 18 tomorrow. But at the end of all of them, the real reason is very simple: I love you because you are Vrushti. And there isn't another you.
